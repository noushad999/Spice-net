# SLIDE 04 — SOCIETAL IMPACT AND MOTIVATION
# Speaker: Maisha | Time: ~1:00

Thank you, Ramim.

Now, let me explain why this project goes beyond just a university assignment.

**Why does spice classification matter?**

**First — Food Safety.**
Spice adulteration is a serious global problem.
Every year, mislabeled and contaminated spices enter food supply chains.
Our system can automatically detect if the wrong spice is in a package —
catching fraud and protecting consumers before the product reaches their kitchen.

**Second — Smart Kitchen Systems.**
We live in a world of smart homes and voice assistants.
Imagine pointing your phone camera at a spice jar
and having it instantly tell you what spice it is — no label needed.
Our model makes that possible.

**Third — Retail and E-Commerce.**
Large spice companies receive hundreds of shipments every week.
Manual checking is slow and expensive.
Our automated system can verify every batch instantly.

**Who benefits?**
Consumers get safer, correctly labeled food.
Food companies get automated quality control.
Retailers get faster, more accurate inventory management.
And researchers get our SpiceSpectrum dataset —
a new, specialized benchmark for food AI work.

**What makes us unique?**
Unlike general food recognition systems that lump spices together —
our system is purpose-built for spices,
with a custom dataset, spice-specific augmentation,
and a model trained specifically for fine-grained spice discrimination.

This is a real solution to a real problem.
