**What we built:**
We developed SpiceFusionNet —
a multi-modal CNN that fuses image, texture, and color features
to classify 11 spice types with high accuracy.

**What we proved:**
Contrastive fine-tuning on hard-negative pairs
significantly reduced confusion between visually similar spices.
The system is suitable for real-time deployment
in smart kitchen, food quality control, and retail use cases.

**The challenges we faced:**
High visual similarity between spice classes made fine-grained discrimination very difficult.
Limited availability of labeled spice data required us to build our own dataset from scratch.
Background clutter was a concern — we addressed it partially with SAM-based segmentation.

**But we overcame every challenge.**
The results speak for themselves.

**Future Work:**
We plan to scale the dataset to 30+ spice categories,
aligned with the SPICE-30 benchmark.
We will deploy the model as a mobile application
using a lightweight MobileNetV3 variant for on-device inference.
And we will extend the system to multi-spice blend recognition
and adulteration detection.

SpiceFusionNet is not the end of this journey — it is the beginning.
